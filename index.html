About the Club

Our Mission

We are a dynamic club dedicated to empowering students with practical skills and knowledge through hands-on training sessions, workshops, and certified courses. Our offerings cover a wide range of fields including web and mobile development, artificial intelligence, DevOps, and more

How We Work

Our Partners

At MTC ENET'Com, we believe in the power of collaboration. Our success is fueled by the support and shared vision of our esteemed partners. Together, we create opportunities for learning, innovation, and growth. We are proud to be associated with organizations that are as passionate about technology and education as we are.

CrocoCoder

a software development training center based in Tunisia. Crococoder offers courses and bootcamps focused on web development, mobile development, and data science, among other areas. They often provide hands-on training, allowing students to work on real projects to build their skills.

2S Training and Consulting

a company that provides training and consulting services, particularly in the fields of IT and business. They offer a range of professional development courses, certifications, and consulting services to help individuals and organizations improve their skills and efficiency.

Enet'Com

ENET'Com, or the National School of Electronics and Telecommunications of Sfax (École Nationale d'Électronique et des Télécommunications de Sfax), is a prestigious engineering school in Tunisia. Part of the University of Sfax, it specializes in electronics, telecommunications, and computer science. As a club within this esteemed institution, MTC ENET'Com benefits from a rich academic environment that drives our innovative projects and technical expertise.
